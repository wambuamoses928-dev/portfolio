What I build

The whole product, not one slice of it.

Products, end to end

Landing page, auth, onboarding, the working app and the admin behind it. One coherent build, not five disconnected pieces.

Payments and wallets

M-Pesa STK push, activation fees, ledgers and withdrawals. Every amount is recomputed on the server before money moves.

Admin and permissions

Roles that hold: read-only accounts that can browse everything, full-access accounts that can act, enforced on every endpoint.

Bots and automation

Telegram bots for alerts, daily numbers and support, plus scheduled jobs that keep queues moving without anyone watching.

Data you can trust

Postgres or Supabase with row-level security and server-side rules, so the dashboard never lies about what happened.

Mobile-first interfaces

Built for the phone first, since that is where the members are. Installable as an app, fast on a thin connection.

How a build runs

Three steps, no theatre.

  1. 1

    Define the outcome

    What the product must do, who pays, what must never break. Agreed before a line of code.

  2. 2

    Build the working thing

    Real backend, real payments in test mode, real admin. Progress lands on a live URL you can open as it happens.

  3. 3

    Ship and keep watch

    Deployed on your domain, alerts wired to your phone, and the fixes that only show up once people use it.
